ScreenBrush alternative for Mac: ScreenBrush vs JellyDraw

Both let you draw over anything on your Mac. They take different approaches, and each is the right choice for different people. Here's an honest side by side.

By the JellyDraw team · Updated October 27, 2026 · We make JellyDraw. Other apps' details come from their own pages, linked below.

Quick answer

ScreenBrush is a long-running Mac App Store app with a lot of presenter extras and support for older Macs. JellyDraw is newer and narrower, built around one thing ScreenBrush doesn't describe: drawings that stick to the window they were drawn on.

Whole screen vs per window

ScreenBrush

Its App Store page says "Draw over the entire screen." Drawings sit on top of the screen as a whole.

JellyDraw

Drawings attach to the app window under them. Move, switch or minimize the window and its drawings go with it.

Where ScreenBrush is the better pick

  • You have an Intel Mac or an older version of macOS. JellyDraw won't run there.
  • You want a laser pointer, flashlight spotlight, magnifier, lower thirds or an iPhone slide remote.
  • You prefer installing through the Mac App Store.

Where JellyDraw fits better

  • You switch between apps while you present, demo or record, and you're tired of drawings floating over the wrong thing.
  • You want a free whiteboard and a simple tool that stays out of the way.
  • You're on Apple Silicon with macOS 26.2 or later.

Questions

Is ScreenBrush free?

The base app is free on the Mac App Store. ScreenBrush Plus is an in-app purchase, $6.99 in the US, with a 7-day trial.

What's the difference between ScreenBrush and JellyDraw?

ScreenBrush draws over the entire screen and has more presenter extras, and it runs on older Macs. JellyDraw attaches each drawing to the window it was drawn on, so drawings move and minimize with their window. It needs Apple Silicon and macOS 26.2 or later.

Does JellyDraw have a laser pointer like ScreenBrush?

It isn't one of JellyDraw's features. Its tools are the pen, highlighter, eraser and whiteboard for free, with shapes, lines, text, export and accent colors in Pro.
